COVID-19 demand collapse — WTI futures briefly settle negative; pump prices hit a decade low near $1.87/gal.

Russia invades Ukraine — Brent tops $130/bbl; national gas average sets a record above $5.00/gal that June.
Feb 28, 2026
United States and Israel strike Iran, opening the 2026 Iran war. The joint operation hit Iranian nuclear and military sites and killed Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei. Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz in response, and crude jumped as roughly a fifth of world oil supply became a shipping risk overnight.
Mar 13, 2026
U.S. strikes Kharg Island, Iran's main oil export terminal, hitting military sites there while avoiding the export infrastructure itself.
Apr–Sep 2026
Naval blockade, ceasefire, and renewed strikes — a U.S. blockade of Iranian oil exports, a conditional ceasefire in April, and fresh U.S.–Iran strikes in late August keep crude and pump prices elevated into fall.

Data source: U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) Open Data API, fetched live each time this page loads. Retail gasoline and diesel prices are weekly U.S. and regional (PADD) averages; WTI and Brent are daily spot prices. Charts show monthly averages from 2010 to the present. EIA typically publishes retail fuel prices on Mondays (delayed by holidays) and spot crude prices daily, so the latest figures reflect the most recent EIA release rather than real-time market quotes. If the live connection fails, the page falls back to an approximate offline reconstruction, flagged by the status badge at the top right.
